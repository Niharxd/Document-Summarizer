CLINICAL CASE SUMMARY

Patient Reference: CASE-2024-0047
Date of Admission: April 10, 2024
Attending Physician: Dr. Eleanor Voss, MD
Department: Internal Medicine

CHIEF COMPLAINT
The patient, a 58-year-old male, presented with a three-week history of progressive shortness
of breath, mild chest discomfort, and bilateral lower-extremity edema.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS
The patient reports gradual onset of dyspnea on exertion over the past three weeks, now
occurring at rest. He denies fever, chills, or productive cough. He has a known history of
hypertension and type 2 diabetes mellitus, managed with lisinopril 10 mg daily and metformin
500 mg twice daily. He is a former smoker with a 20-pack-year history, having quit six years ago.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION
- Vital Signs: BP 148/92 mmHg, HR 96 bpm, RR 20/min, SpO2 91% on room air, Temp 37.1°C
- Cardiovascular: Regular rate and rhythm, S3 gallop present, JVD noted at 45 degrees
- Respiratory: Bibasilar crackles on auscultation
- Extremities: 2+ pitting edema bilateral ankles

INVESTIGATIONS
- ECG: Sinus tachycardia, left bundle branch block (new compared to prior ECG from 2022)
- Chest X-Ray: Cardiomegaly, bilateral pleural effusions, pulmonary vascular congestion
- BNP: 1,840 pg/mL (elevated)
- Echocardiogram: Ejection fraction 32%, global hypokinesis, dilated left ventricle

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN
Primary Diagnosis: Acute decompensated heart failure (HFrEF), NYHA Class III
Secondary Diagnosis: Hypertension, Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

Management:
1. IV furosemide 40 mg twice daily for diuresis; monitor daily weights and fluid balance
2. Initiate carvedilol 3.125 mg twice daily; uptitrate as tolerated
3. Continue lisinopril; consider uptitration after stabilisation
4. Cardiology consult requested
5. Diabetic diet; endocrinology review for glycaemic optimisation
6. Patient education regarding fluid restriction and daily weight monitoring

PROGNOSIS
Guarded. Patient will require close outpatient follow-up and optimisation of guideline-directed
medical therapy. Referral for cardiac rehabilitation to be considered at discharge.
